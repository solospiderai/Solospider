Key Monetization Insight

The unit economics are built around prompt slots (150/300) and generated articles (3/8 per month). These are the natural consumption limits. API costs for running prompts across 5 models ~$0.01-0.05/prompt. At 300 prompts/day = ~$3-15/day in LLM costs. Article generation with research = ~$0.50-2/article. Margins are very healthy at $499/month.
